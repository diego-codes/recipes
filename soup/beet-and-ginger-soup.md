# Beet and ginger soup

For those seeking a sweet, spicy, salty dinner - this soup might be your answer.
This recipe is based a family recipe from Meera Sodha, the author of [East](https://bookshop.org/p/books/east-120-vegan-and-vegetarian-recipes-from-bangalore-to-beijing-american-measurements-meera-sodha/13704398).


## Ingredients for 4 servings

- 1 pound of raw beets, scrubbed and grated
- 1 small potato, peeled and grated
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 3/4 inch of ginger, peeled and grated
- 3 cloves of garlic, crushed
- 1 serrano chile pepper, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp of canola oil
- 3 cups vegetable stock
- Salt, to taste
- Optional, plain Greek yogurt
- Optional, sesame seeds
- Optional, grilled sourdough bread


## Instructions

1. Chop or grate 1 pound of beets and the small potato.

2. Chop up the medium onion.

3. Separately, peel and grate the ginger. Then finely chop it up with the garlic and serrano chile pepper.

4. In a large pot, heat the 3 tbsp of canola oil. Once it's ready, fry the chopped onion for 10 minutes. If you want to let some of it caramelize a bit, all power to you.

5. Add the chopped up ginger, garlic, and serrano pepper to the pot to fry for 3 minutes, stirring everything the whole time.

6. At this point, add the beets and potatoes, stirring to coat them in the fried veggies. Then add in the veggie stock and bring to a boil.

7. Once boiling, turn down the heat and have it simmer for 30 minutes.

8. Blend everything and taste. Add a dash of salt if desired.

9. Ladle the soup into bowls and add some yogurt and a dash of sesame seeds on top.

10. Grill the sourdough bread and serve alongside soup.
